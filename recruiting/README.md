# Recruiting

**In politics, the leader recruits the candidates. That is the key fix.**

Parties hold power less through ideas than through recruiting: they spot talent early, make the ask, and promise the machine. This folder replaces that function — openly, and without the party.

## Contents

- [`candidate-scorecard.md`](candidate-scorecard.md) — how candidates are vetted: track record, local trust, delivery evidence, integrity. Ideology is not scored.
- [`target-districts.md`](target-districts.md) — how the first 25–50 target districts are chosen and scored, in public.

## Principles

- **Recruit, don't wait.** Great local leaders rarely self-nominate. A named person makes a personal ask.
- **Track record over tribe.** Mayors who fixed things, owners who met payroll, veterans who led, organizers who delivered — from any background or none.
- **The candidate is not ours.** Recruiting creates no ownership, no oath, and no debt. See the [steering charter](../steering/).
- **Declines stay private.** No public scorecards, shortlists, or personal data for anyone who has not publicly announced.
