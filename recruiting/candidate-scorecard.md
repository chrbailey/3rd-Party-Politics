# Candidate Scorecard

We recruit people who have *already done the job in miniature*: run something, delivered something, been trusted by people who know them. Charisma and ideology are not scored.

Score each criterion 0–3. A recruiting conversation is warranted at **14+**, with no zero on Track Record or Integrity.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Track record** | Never run anything | Helped run something | Ran a team / business / unit | Ran something significant, measurably well |
| **Local trust** | Unknown locally | Known in a niche | Known and respected across groups | The person locals name first |
| **Delivery evidence** | Claims only | Anecdotes | Documents / budgets / results | Independently verifiable results |
| **Integrity** | Red flags | Unclear | Clean public record | Clean record + handles bad news openly |
| **Reason to run** | Fame / revenge | General frustration | A specific local problem | A specific problem they have already worked on |
| **Commitment fit** | Wants a platform | Vague promises | Will sign a written commitment | Drafts specific, checkable commitments unprompted |
| **Stamina** | Untested | Some hard seasons | Has done long, hard campaigns of work | Has done this exact kind of grind before |

## Automatic disqualifiers

- Wants the run mainly as media / brand leverage
- Refuses a public, written Constituent Commitment
- Refuses financial disclosure beyond what the law requires
- Expects the platform to set their positions — or to be set *by* them
- Any credible, unresolved integrity issue (fraud, abuse, corruption)

## How a recruiting conversation goes

1. **The ask is personal.** A named steering-group member makes it, in person where possible. Talent is recruited, not advertised for.
2. **Lead with the rails.** "You would not have to build the machine. Here is exactly what is handled, and what is yours."
3. **Lead with the exit right.** "You own everything. You can leave anytime. There is no oath."
4. **Ask for the commitment, not agreement.** The only document they sign is *to their constituents*.
5. **Accept no gracefully.** A good "not now" is a future candidate and a present validator. Log it, thank them, move on.

## Privacy

Scorecards for people who decline are **not** published and no private information is ever committed to this repo. Only recruited, publicly announced candidates get a public page — written with them.
