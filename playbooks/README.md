# Playbooks

The rails, written down. Each playbook is a checklist a real candidate team can run — in order, under deadline, at 11pm.

## Playbooks

- [`launch-checklist.md`](launch-checklist.md) — from "yes, I'll run" to a legal, funded, announced campaign: the first 14 days

## Coming next (open an issue to claim one)

- **Filing week** — forms, signatures audit, fee, notarization, the trip to the registrar
- **Compliance calendar** — every federal/state report, deadline, and disclaimer, in one calendar
- **Small-donor fundraising** — stack setup, first-100-donors plan, transparent reporting
- **Distribution without a party** — debates, local media, podcasts, town halls, short video, email list you own
- **Signature gathering** — circulator recruiting, validity rates, tracking, buffer math (always gather 150%+)
- **Post-mortem** — the template every supported campaign publishes after election day, win or lose

## Writing a playbook

- Checklists over essays. Verbs first. One owner per step.
- Include the *real* deadline and the *safe* deadline (they are never the same).
- Name the failure you have personally seen. Scar tissue is the curriculum.
- No legal advice cosplay: mark clearly where the candidate needs a real election lawyer, and what to ask them.
