# Contributing

Welcome. This project succeeds if ordinary, competent people can do small, verifiable pieces of work that add up to real campaign infrastructure.

## The highest-value contributions right now

1. **Verify a state** — Pick a state in `ballot-access/`, check its independent-candidate rules against the *official* Secretary of State / State Board of Elections source, and submit the file or correction. Every factual claim needs a source link and a `last_verified` date. No source, no merge.
2. **Score a district** — Follow `recruiting/target-districts.md`. Show your data and sources; scoring is a method, not a vibe.
3. **Add a playbook step** — Ran a campaign, a small business, a compliance function, an event? Turn what you learned into a checklist step someone else can follow.
4. **Stress-test the guardrails** — Open an issue arguing how this could quietly become a party, and what design change would prevent it. These are prized contributions.

## Ground rules

- **Facts need sources.** Election rules change. Cite the official source and date your verification.
- **Process, not ideology.** Contributions that push a policy platform, a candidate's positions, or partisan attacks don't belong here — there are plenty of repos for that.
- **No private data.** Never commit voter files, donor lists, personal contact details, or anything you wouldn't want on the front page.
- **Plain language wins.** A checklist a busy local candidate can follow at 11pm beats a white paper.
- **Be kind and specific.** See the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How to submit

1. Fork the repo and branch from `main` (`state-verify-ohio`, `playbook-filing-week`, …)
2. Make one focused change per PR
3. In the PR description: what you changed, what source(s) you used, and what you did *not* verify
4. Keep discussion in the PR — decisions and their reasons should be public by default

## Suggesting a candidate or a district

Use the issue templates. Public suggestions only — do not post anyone's private contact information, and do not suggest someone without a plausible public basis (they've held office, run something visible, led something real).

## Questions

Open a Discussion or an issue labeled `question`. There are no dumb questions about ballot access — the rules are genuinely confusing, which is rather the point of this repo.
